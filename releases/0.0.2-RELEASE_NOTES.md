Dead Sector 0.0.2 packages the game as one self-contained HTML file for desktop Chrome. Extract `0.0.2.zip`, double-click `DeadSector.html`, and click **ENTER THE SECTOR**. No Python, server, installation, or internet connection is required to play. Chrome with WebGL2, a keyboard, and a mouse are required.

- English and Simplified Chinese menus and HUD.
- Rifle, pistol, and shotgun with independent ammunition and reloading.
- Slow-moving zombies, survival waves, smooth 3D characters, footsteps, and distinct gunshots.
- Adjustable graphics, audio volume, and mouse sensitivity.

Downloads: `0.0.2.zip` is the playable package; `DeadSector-0.0.2.html` is the standalone file; `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums.

Validation carried forward from the standalone build: 13 simulation tests, four browser tests, and two offline embedded-content checks passed. Direct `file://` tests were blocked before startup by the cloud browser's administrator policy. No successful direct-file launch test or hardware-specific frame-rate benchmark is claimed.
